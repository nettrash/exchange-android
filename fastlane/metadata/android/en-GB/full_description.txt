Exchange brings end-to-end encryption to the messenger you already use. Type a message, pick a recipient whose public key you have on file, and Exchange hands back a single base64 line — paste it into Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail, or anything else that carries text. Only your recipient can read it. The signature proves it came from you.

How it works
- Each message gets a fresh ephemeral X25519 key.
- ECDH against your recipient's static encryption key, mixed through HKDF-SHA256, derives a per-message wrapping key.
- A random 32-byte message key is wrapped with ChaCha20-Poly1305 under that key.
- The plaintext is encrypted with ChaCha20-Poly1305 under the message key.
- The whole packed envelope is signed with your long-term Ed25519 key.
- Out comes EXC2:<base64> — one line you can paste anywhere.

On the receiving side Exchange verifies the signature first, then the cheap "is this for me?" recipient fingerprint, then unwraps the message key, then decrypts the body — in that exact order, so a tampered envelope never touches your private key.

Exchanging public keys
- Show your QR code in person and let the other device scan it.
- Or copy your public-key bundle as base64 and paste it through any channel you trust.
- Every key is stamped with an 8-byte fingerprint shown in groups of four hex characters. Verify it out of band before sending anything sensitive.

Backup and device transfer
- Passphrase backup (EXCBKP1) — exports your identity and recipient list as one encrypted base64 line, derived through 600,000 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 iterations. Save it in a password manager, restore it on the next device.
- In-person identity QR (EXCQR1) — fast one-shot transfer between two devices you hold. The whole identity is encrypted with a fresh symmetric key embedded in the QR itself; keep the QR visible only to the receiving device.

System integration
- Share Sheet — Exchange registers as a target for shared text, so a pasted envelope can be sent straight from Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, or any messenger that supports share-out.
- App Links — taps on https://exchange.nettrash.me/msg/... links open directly into the decrypted message.
- Adaptive launcher icon, themed icon on Android 13+, Material 3 dynamic colour.

Private by design
- 100% on-device. No accounts, no servers, no telemetry, no ads, no trackers.
- Your identity's private half is stored in the Android Keystore, wrapped under an AES-256/GCM key held in the device's hardware-backed keystore (TEE or StrongBox where supported).
- Recipients live in a local SQLite database; nothing about them ever leaves the device.
- The app does not request the INTERNET permission. It makes no outbound calls.

Cross-platform
Exchange is byte-compatible with the iOS app of the same name. An envelope sealed on iPhone opens on Android and vice versa.

Requires Android 16 (API 36) or later.
