Exchange 1.3 — quicker to compose, easier to decrypt.

• Compose remembers who you last messaged and pre-selects them — no more scrolling back to the same person every time.
• The recipient picker now follows the same order as your home-screen list, instead of sorting alphabetically.
• Decrypt now takes a shared message link, not just the raw EXC2: text — paste either and it just works.
• When sharing a message you can now choose the format: the rich link or the raw EXC2: text. Your choice is remembered.
• New optional App Lock: require your fingerprint, face, or device PIN before Exchange opens, with configurable re-lock timing and scope.
• Encrypt and decrypt files: seal any file to a recipient as a shareable .exc2, and restore the original on the other side. Works across Android, iOS and macOS.
• Still no accounts, no servers, no telemetry, no ads.
